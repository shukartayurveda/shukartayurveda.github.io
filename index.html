Most chronic problems, from acidity and fatty liver to joint pain and poor sleep, keep coming back because only the surface is treated. Ayurveda asks a different question: why did this imbalance start, and what keeps feeding it?

About Shukart Ayurveda

Panchakarma and therapies

Panchakarma is a medical process, not a spa menu.

Each therapy is chosen after assessment, prepared for properly, supervised through the procedure, and followed by the right diet and post-therapy care.

Ask about Panchakarma

Abhyanga

Therapeutic full-body oil application, with oils chosen for you and your treatment goal.

Swedana

Supervised herbal steam, used to prepare the body or support other therapies.

Basti

A classical Panchakarma procedure whose type and schedule are set by the physician.

Nasya

Medicated nasal therapy for conditions of the head, neck and senses.

Shirodhara

A steady stream of warm oil on the forehead, used for stress, sleep and mental calm.

Virechana

Guided therapeutic purgation, advised only when suitable after careful preparation.

Before your visit

General answers. The doctor will advise what is right for you after assessing your case.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Your prescriptions, test reports, the list of medicines you currently take, and a short history of your main concern. This keeps the consultation accurate and saves time.

Is Panchakarma suitable for everyone?

No. It depends on your age, strength, diagnosis, stage of illness and other health factors. The doctor will assess you before recommending any procedure.

Can I take Ayurvedic and allopathic medicines together?

Please don't stop or combine medicines on your own. Share your full medicine list during consultation so the doctor can advise safely and coordinate with your other doctors if needed.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes, for suitable cases. Some conditions may still need a physical examination at the clinic.

How long does Ayurvedic treatment take?

It depends on the condition, how long you have had it, your strength and how closely the plan is followed. You'll get a realistic timeline after assessment.
